# Ordinary Hierophant boundaries

The ordinary Default/HardMode identity gates Surge, mana cost, mana-derived damage/area and active-totem sources.
Surge availability is captured before actual Mana payment in the shared ordinary-use receipt.
Scheduled releases retain that value; autonomous Brand, totem and Mirage activations start unpaid scopes.
A hit that grants Surge does not change the other victims of that activation.

## Supported actors

Storm Brand and Armageddon Brand use separate cumulative attached and detached duration budgets.
The base budgets are five and six seconds; native binary32 modifier order also applies to published durations.
Recall joins its base cost and the recalled brands' current cost shares in one resource/cooldown admission.
The original executable ticket prevents replay payment; Recall keeps the existing duration budgets.
Fireball, Ice Nova and Freezing Pulse with Spell Totem, and intrinsic Shockwave Totem, have actual actor consumers.
Unsupported linked Arc and Frostbolt refuse before payment.
Totems retain body regeneration, lifetime and source cleanup without owner leech, charge or critical-flask rewards.
Brands retain owner rewards and recipient conditions.

## Bounded retained Brand presentation

The default attachment buff contexts are MiscAnimated row/selector pairs `[4478, 0]` for Storm Brand
and `[4479, 0]` for Armageddon Brand.
The ActiveSigil consumer reads both entries; selector zero selects the normal attachment animation.
Retained action `0x2c6` keeps the action type, skill, sequence and clone identity together.
The bounded first clone has identity two; later allocation and nondefault producers are not inferred.
The target buff precedes the retained attachment in one output group.
Detach, expiry, source replacement and retirement remove only the owned presentation.
Live target buff holders and pending ground/DoT outputs reserve independent instances through allocator wrap.
Counted packet leases end at the common send gate or explicit discard, including reload and retirement.
